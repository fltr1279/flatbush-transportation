FAQ

Common questions.

Is Flatbush Transportation a public car service?

No. The core program is a private nonprofit service for participating families connected to the broader kollel/community project.

Where does the school transportation operate?

The main children's corridor is Flatbush to Boro Park and back.

Can I request transportation if I am not on a regular route?

You can submit a request. It is reviewed based on availability and whether it can fit without disrupting the main program.

Can I rent a van?

When a vehicle is available outside school/kollel use, a private rental request can be submitted.

Will parents be able to track vans?

Yes. The planned parent app is intended to connect with the existing GeoTrack fleet system.

Are children's routes public?

No. Names, addresses, schedules and exact live van locations belong behind secure family access.
