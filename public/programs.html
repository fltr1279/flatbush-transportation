CHILDREN & MOSDOS

Flatbush to Boro Park — and home again.

Participating families receive transportation for boys, girls, bachurim and younger children attending heimishe mosdos. Morning and return routes operate Sunday through Friday whenever regular school is in session.

We work hard to keep the system practical for families while protecting the integrity of the main route. When possible, older boys and girls are arranged separately.

KOLLEL TRANSPORTATION

80+ yungerlait moved every day.

Transportation is also part of the kollel infrastructure. More than 80 yungerlait are transported daily to the kollel and back, making it easier for the learning schedule and family life to work together.
