OPERATING POLICIES

Clear expectations make transportation work.

Draft operational language for organizational review. These policies should be reviewed before being treated as final legal or contractual terms.

Route priority

The regular child and kollel transportation programs have priority over optional requests and rentals.

Requests and approvals

Transportation changes and rental submissions are requests only until the office confirms them.

Pickup readiness

Riders and families should be ready at the agreed pickup location and time.

Conduct

Children are expected to remain seated, follow driver instructions, respect other riders and treat the vehicle as they would their own family vehicle.

Capacity

No route or rental may exceed the legal/approved passenger capacity of the assigned vehicle.

Rental documentation

Rental approval may require identification, driver information, insurance, deposit or other documentation.

Privacy

Child information, family addresses, schedules, ID documents and live vehicle location data are private information.
