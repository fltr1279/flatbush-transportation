VAN RENTALS

When a van is available, request it.

Evenings, weekends and holidays may create availability for private family and community transportation needs.

Rental requests are reviewed individually.

School and kollel transportation always comes first. A request does not reserve a vehicle until the office confirms availability and any required documentation, driver or insurance arrangements.

  1. Choose exact pickup and return date/time.
  2. Pin starting point and destination on Google Maps.
  3. Upload your ID or requested driver documentation.
  4. Submit — the office receives the request directly by email and in the request database.
