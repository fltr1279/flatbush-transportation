WHY THIS EXISTS

Transportation was never the goal.
Building Torah life was.

Rabbi Benzion Frankel's vision is to strengthen Torah and Chassidish family life in Flatbush. The kollel project brought yungerlait and their families into the neighborhood with real support for the practical challenges of building a life here.

Transportation became one of those promises: children should be able to attend the heimishe mosdos their families choose, and yungerlait should be able to get to the kollel reliably. Flatbush Transportation grew from that need.
