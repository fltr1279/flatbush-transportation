PRIVACY

Private information stays private.

Draft privacy language for review. A lawyer or privacy professional should review it before final publication.

Information we may collect

Transportation requests may include names, contact details, pickup/destination information and schedule details. Rental requests may also include identification documents and trip information.

Why we collect it

Information is used to review requests, coordinate transportation, communicate with families/renters, operate routes and maintain safety.

ID documents

Uploaded identification is stored in private object storage and is not intended to be publicly accessible.

Children's information

Names, addresses, schools, schedules and live van locations should never be displayed publicly.

Third-party systems

The organization may use providers for fleet tracking, maps, email delivery, hosting, storage and payments.
